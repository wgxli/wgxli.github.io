BBB-smooth integers with constrained factors

Published February 27, 2026｜5 minute read

As part of a project, I needed to compute the probability of a large integer being BBB-smooth, conditional on knowledge about the sizes of its prime factors. I found very little information about this problem in the literature.

This post (1) solves this problem with high accuracy, and (2) verifies the theoretical results experimentally.

Problem Statement
